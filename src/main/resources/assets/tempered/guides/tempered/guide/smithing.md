# Apply an Aspect

Use a found [Aspect](loot.md) and compatible equipment at a **smithing table**.

1. **Left slot — template:** leave empty.
2. **Middle slot — base:** put in your equipment.
3. **Right slot — addition:** put in one Aspect.
4. Check the result, then take it: **one Aspect adds one level**.

Hold **Shift** over your equipment to check compatibility and the next level.

## No result?

Check that the left slot is empty, the item accepts the Aspect, the effect is
enabled on this server, and the application cap has not been reached.
The Shift tooltip explains the equipment's upgrade status.

## What stays on the equipment?

Applying an Aspect keeps enchantments, names, earned Affix progress and other
item data. Previewing or removing the ingredients does not change them.

Each level's bonus is the **total bonus**, not an extra percentage added to
the previous level. Reinforced increases maximum durability; it does not fully
repair the item.

For netherite upgrades, anvil repairs, armor trims and disenchanting, follow
[Upgrades and repairs](maintenance.md).
