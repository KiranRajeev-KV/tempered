# Applying Aspects

Apply found [Aspects](loot.md) at a **smithing table**:

1. **Template slot (left):** leave empty.
2. **Base slot (middle):** put in your equipment.
3. **Addition slot (right):** put in an Aspect.
4. Take the result: **one Aspect adds one level**.

<ItemLink id="tempered:reinforced_aspect" /> accepts any item with durability.
<ItemLink id="tempered:swift_aspect" /> accepts compatible mining tools, usually
pickaxes, axes, shovels and hoes. Hold **Shift** over the equipment to check
its upgrade eligibility and current cap.

## What the upgrade preserves

Upgrading keeps enchantments, the custom name and other item data. Reinforced
raises maximum durability; it does **not** fully repair the item. Each listed
bonus is the **total at that level**, rather than an extra bonus per application.

## No result?

Check that the template slot is empty, the equipment is compatible, the Aspect
is enabled on this server, and its application cap has not been reached.
The Shift tooltip explains the equipment's upgrade status.
