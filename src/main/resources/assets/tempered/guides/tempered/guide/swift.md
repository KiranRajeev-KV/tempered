# Swift

<ItemImage id="tempered:swift_aspect" float="left" />

Increase **mining speed** on compatible tools, usually pickaxes, axes, shovels
and hoes. [Find an Aspect](loot.md), then
[apply it at a smithing table](smithing.md).
Hold **Shift** over a tool to check whether it accepts Swift.

## Active levels

<tempered:Settings topic="swift" />

## How the bonus works

Swift multiplies final mining speed, including Efficiency and Haste.
A listed bonus is the **total at that level**, not an extra percentage per application.

Your server's datapacks determine which tools accept Swift. Incompatible
equipment keeps its history but receives no Swift effect.

## Keeping your levels

[Supported material upgrades and repairs](maintenance.md) retain Swift levels.
Disabling the effect or lowering its cap also keeps them; the active bonus uses
the current cap. Raising the cap or re-enabling Swift makes that history
available again.

See [server configuration](configuration.md) to tune these rules.
