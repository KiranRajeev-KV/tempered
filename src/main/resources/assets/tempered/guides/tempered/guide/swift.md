# Swift

<ItemImage id="tempered:swift_aspect" float="left" />

The Swift Aspect increases **mining speed**. It accepts Swift-compatible tools,
usually pickaxes, axes, shovels and hoes. [Find it as loot](loot.md), then
[apply it at smithing](smithing.md).

## How the bonus works

Swift multiplies final mining speed, including Efficiency and Haste. Listed
bonuses are **totals at each level**, not extra bonuses per application.

Compatibility is controlled by the server's datapacks through the
`tempered:swift_applicable` item tag. Hold **Shift** over equipment to check
whether it accepts Swift.

## Configured levels

<tempered:Settings topic="swift" />

Saved levels above a lowered cap are retained; the active bonus uses the cap.
Disabling Swift keeps that history. Raising the cap or re-enabling the mechanic
makes it available again.
